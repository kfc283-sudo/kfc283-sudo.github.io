Privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Contact Scanner is a personal tool used by one person (the owner) for their own contacts. Only the owner's Google account can sign in.

What it accesses

  • Google Contacts: to find the owner's existing contacts, and to create or update a contact from a business card the owner has confirmed.
  • Google Drive: to read card photos the owner uploads to a "Card inbox" folder, and to file confirmed card images in the owner's "Business Card" folder.

How data is used

  • Card photos are sent to an AI model on Amazon Bedrock to read the text on the card. Amazon Bedrock does not use them to train models.
  • Data stays in the owner's own Google account. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.
  • The tool keeps no copy of contacts or photos outside the owner's Google account, apart from short-lived working copies while a card is being read.

Google user data

Contact Scanner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
