Contact Scanner

A personal tool that reads business cards and saves the people to its owner's Google Contacts.

It is used by one person only, for their own contacts. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in.

How it works

  1. The owner photographs business cards and uploads the photos to a folder in their own Google Drive.
  2. An AI model reads the text on each card.
  3. The owner checks each reading on a review page and confirms it.
  4. On confirmation, the contact is created or updated in the owner's Google Contacts, and the card image is filed in the owner's Google Drive.
